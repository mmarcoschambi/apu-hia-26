# Experiment Report: EXP-010 Dynamic ADR Stop

## Overview
**Experiment ID:** EXP-010  
**Hypothesis:** Using a dynamic stop based on 50% of the 14-day ADR (`min(8%, 0.5 * ADR_14)`) will reduce risk and improve the Profit Factor by avoiding large drawdowns in volatile stocks and capturing tighter entries.  
**Date:** 2026-05-13  
**Status:** **REJECTED (NO-GO)**

- **Engine:** Integrated 5 stop modes and adaptive sizing into `simulate_fast_core` (Numba).

- **Runner:** Created `experiments/dynamic_adr_stop_exp010.py` for walk-forward validation.

### Key Observations
1. **Stop Distance too tight:** The average stop distance was reduced from 8.0% to ~1.4%. This was insufficient to survive intraday noise, leading to immediate stop-outs of winning trades.
2. **Phantom Stop Explosion:** 60-75% of trades stopped out would have reached TP1 if given the baseline room.
3. **Variant B3 (Reject < 2%)** was slightly more stable but still underperformed the fixed 8% baseline.
4. **ATR 1.5x (Variant C)** was the only dynamic alternative that didn't catastrophically fail, though it didn't clearly beat the 8% fixed stop in this specific setup.

## Final Decision
**NO-GO for production promotion.**  
The 0.5x ADR multiplier is too aggressive for the `pocket_pivot` / `combo_pullback` entry style. The noise floor of these tickers is higher than the calculated stops.

## Future Recommendations
- Test a wider multiplier: `1.0x` or `1.25x` ADR.
- Combine ADR stop with a hard floor of at least 3% (Variant E showed better recovery than B1).
- Use Dynamic ADR stops only for "Tight Consolidation" setups where the entry is closer to the pivot.
